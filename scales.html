Governance scales

How participatory, transparent and hierarchical each governance option is, from 1 to 5: the scores behind every published card's scales, here for the team to review. Not linked from the site.

The scales

ScaleWhat it measuresLowHigh

None of the scales is “good” or “bad”: many communities choose hierarchy, or privacy, on purpose. The scales describe; they don't judge.

In the tables below, P, T and H are Participatory, Transparent and Hierarchical.

How a card's scores work

  • A card's score on each scale is the average of the scores of what it chose: its decision-making approaches, how people join (tiers and ways) and its conflict management approaches, rounded to a whole number. The card says what it's based on (e.g. “Participatory 4 / 5 · based on 6 choices”).
  • — means an option says nothing about that scale; it's left out of the average (not counted as low).
  • If nothing a card chose is scored on a scale, that scale shows N/A.
  • Only accepted scores count; proposed ones are drafts waiting for review.
  • Later, the same scores can suggest approaches: a community says what it wants on each scale, and the approaches whose scores are closest are suggested (with these reasons as the explanation).

Reviewing

For each option, check its three scores and its reason (the reason may be quoted to communities, so it should read well on its own). Send changes to Sohyeon, or edit data/governance_scales.json directly; once a score is agreed, its status becomes accepted. This page always shows the file as it is.
